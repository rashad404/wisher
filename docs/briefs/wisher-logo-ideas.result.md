# Wisher logo ideas result

Review page: http://100.89.150.50:3022/concepts/logo-ideas.html

Nine concepts, all unselected:

1. Pomegranate wishes - Azerbaijani abundance and good wishes, with three large seeds.
2. A day circled - one important day singled out by a hand-drawn circle.
3. Kept in mind - a folded page corner marking something worth remembering.
4. Held close - open arms put the recipient at the center.
5. A wish on its way - an angular paper bird carries a personal note.
6. Make a wish - the pause before blowing out a birthday candle.
7. A personal signature - a free-standing handwritten W gesture, without a tile or typeset serif monogram.
8. Words for you - two opening quotation marks celebrate the personal message.
9. A wish unfolding - a seed held in an opening leaf.

## Design review notes

The equal specimen layouts are intentional: every icon receives the same comparison conditions. They are not a proposed customer-facing card grid. No marketing promises, made-up evidence or sample customer content was added. The page's task is choosing by number, rather than a sales conversion.

All nine silhouettes remain visible at 16px. Recognition of their intended meaning still needs Rashad's judgment: the circle can read as a target, the folded page as a document, the embrace as care, and the leaf as growth. These are conceptual tradeoffs, not claimed proof of exclusive ownership. No existing brand mark was used as a reference or copied; trademark clearance was not performed.

The old rust/red tiles, eight-point star, w gift tag, heart speech bubble and ribbon bow were not repeated. The handwritten W is a free-standing custom brush shape, an explicit territory allowed by the brief, rather than a variation of the rejected serif tile.

No site header, hero, favicon, existing logo asset or rejected review page was changed. The approved page remains as it was.
