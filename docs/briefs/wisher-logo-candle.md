Rashad's idea (2026-11-13), building on concept 06 "Make a wish": the "i" in "wisher" is a birthday candle and its dot is the flame. The candle alone becomes the app icon and favicon.

The lead's quick sketch, for reference only (not final): http://100.89.150.50:3022/concepts/wish-candle.html (three flame treatments: white, amber with a light core, amber with a glow). Your previous review page: http://100.89.150.50:3022/concepts/logo-ideas.html. Approved page direction: http://100.89.150.50:3022/az/concepts/d (pomegranate red #a3122f, leaf green #0f6b4f, Fraunces wordmark).

## Deliver
1. A properly drawn wordmark as clean vector paths (not live text): "wisher" with the candle-i and flame, plus ".az". Refine letter spacing, the candle stem (should it be slightly taller than the x-height, or a plain i stem?), flame shape and its position so it reads as a candle at first glance and still reads as "wisher". Try 3-5 serious variations of the candle-i (stem width/height, flame shape, white vs warm amber flame, optional tiny wick, with/without glow).
2. Matching icons: app icon (1024), favicon at 16/32/48 and an SVG favicon, Apple touch icon. Make the 16 px version simple enough to read (simplify for small sizes if needed).
3. Show on red and on white, in the header at real size, and as a phone home-screen icon mockup.
4. One review page at frontend/public/concepts/candle-logo.html, numbered variants, so Rashad can pick. Stop there; do not install into the site.

Rules: original drawing, no copying other brands; plain ASCII in docs; commit locally; no deploy. Result: docs/briefs/wisher-logo-candle.result.md with the URL.
