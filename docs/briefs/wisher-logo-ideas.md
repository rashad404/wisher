# Brief: Wisher logo icon ideas

Rashad needs a logo icon (and favicon) for Wisher.az. Only ideas this round: show concepts, do not build them into the site.

## Context
- Wisher.az helps people in Azerbaijan remember the special days of people they love, find a gift from Azerbaijani stores, and send a personal greeting written with AI help. Warm, personal, celebratory, Azerbaijani; selling to normal families, not tech people.
- The approved page direction: http://100.89.150.50:3022/az/concepts/d (header and hero approved by Rashad). Colours: pomegranate red #a3122f (brand), leaf green #0f6b4f (action), white. Headline font Fraunces (bold serif), text Manrope. The wordmark "wisher" in Fraunces with a small ".az" works; the ICON is the problem.
- Read the product-design skill (logo: simple, recognisable at 16 px, no gradients, no sparkle/AI symbols, no generic gift box with bow).

## Rejected by Rashad (do not repeat or lightly vary)
- The old rust rounded square with a "w".
- An 8-point star (looked like a settings gear), with or without a hole.
- Four options shown on http://100.89.150.50:3022/concepts/logos.html: A bold serif "w" monogram in a red tile, B gift tag with "w", C speech bubble with a heart, D ribbon bow. His words: "super bad", "not even close".

## Deliverable
1. 8-10 genuinely different concepts. Think like a brand designer: what one idea can only belong to Wisher? (For example: the moment of being remembered, a name, a date, a wish, a pomegranate as an Azerbaijani symbol of abundance and good wishes, a hand-written touch, a custom "w" ligature, negative space...). Each concept needs a one-line rationale.
2. Use the imagegen skill to explore, then redraw the best directions as clean vector SVG (flat, 1-2 colours) so they work as a favicon.
3. One review page at frontend/public/concepts/logo-ideas.html showing every concept: icon at 16, 32, 64 and 180 px, on red and on white, and as a lockup with the "wisher.az" wordmark on the red header. Number them so Rashad can pick by number.
4. Stop after the page is ready. Report the URL (http://100.89.150.50:3022/concepts/logo-ideas.html) and a short list of the concepts.

Rules: no copying existing brands' marks; plain ASCII in docs; commit locally; no deploy.
