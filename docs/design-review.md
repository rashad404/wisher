# Approved concept D review

Read the full product-design skill and modern-vs-template.md before implementation. Reviewed the actual 1440 and 375 renders in docs/screenshots/apply-design/approved-*.png.

## Section 3

- Confident: passes. Pomegranate stage, large serif headline and green action are decisive.
- Tangible: passes. A named greeting card and a priced retail gift carry the first screen.
- Specific: passes. Azerbaijani occasions, local retailer inventory and personal memories belong here.
- Clear hierarchy: mostly passes. Gift discovery leads; greeting is secondary. Clarify that browsing needs no login, while generation and saving do.
- Varied rhythm: passes. Stage, occasion tiles, shop grid, writing example and inline reminder have different forms.
- Restrained chrome: mostly passes. Flat controls; shadows support raised cards. Keep the approved treatment.
- Crafted details: incomplete. Concept badges/labels reach 12px and hearts/chips are under 44px. Mobile overflows in greeting and closing sections; the third gift breaks the two-column grid. Correct these implementation defects.
- Alive, not busy: passes. Small card tilts and occasion objects provide enough movement; respect reduced motion.
- Fast and honest: incomplete. Samples are labelled, but save buttons do nothing, filters lose selections and reminder fields are discarded. Wire these to existing flows; use live catalog data with loading/error/empty states. Do not imply SMS delivery or prepared gifts exist automatically.

## Section 4

- Empty centred hero: absent; the split stage contains a tangible greeting.
- Three generic feature cards: absent; three greeting outputs demonstrate distinct tones.
- Unsourced proof strips/reviews: absent.
- Decorative dashboard: absent.
- Beige/rust/gold/default-blue/purple-gradient system: absent. White, red and green are distinctive.
- Repeated eyebrows/tiny grey metadata: no repeated eyebrows; useful store metadata remains, with a 13px floor.
- Identical section cards/heights/arrows: absent; sections differ by purpose.
- Stock people/clip-art/heavy button shadows: no stock people or button shadows. Approved occasion assets form a consistent licensed family, retained as requested.
- Interchangeable copy: mostly absent; the promise, named greeting and local shopping are specific. Keep approved wording except necessary truth corrections noted above.

The desktop look is approved, not an invitation to redesign. Improvements are functional wiring, accessible control sizes, truthful scope and the expressly requested mobile composition. Gifts gets only a desktop first screen, pending approval.
