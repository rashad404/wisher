Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one exploratory art-direction contact sheet, a 3 by 3 board of nine distinctly different icon ideas for Wisher.az. This board is research for subsequent manual vector drawing, not final assets.
Brand: helps ordinary Azerbaijani families remember loved ones' dates, find personal gifts, and write heartfelt greetings. Warm and personal, not technology.
Palette: flat pomegranate red #a3122f on white only. Generous white space. Black small number labels 1 through 9 outside each mark. No wordmarks. All marks must be bold simple silhouettes or thick open strokes that can survive at 16 pixels.
Explore these nine conceptual territories with craft and originality:
1. A pomegranate cut open: a simple three-prong crown and three large seeds, Azerbaijan and abundance, not an intricate fruit drawing.
2. A remembered date: one thick almost-complete hand-drawn circle enclosing one bold dot, a tiny intentional opening in the circle, not a power-button symbol.
3. A page corner folded to remember someone: a sharp asymmetrical page silhouette, one large curved negative-space fold, no calendar rings or tiny numbers.
4. An embrace: two protective opposing curved arms enclosing a small person/dot, no heart shape.
5. A wish taking flight: a simple paper bird, two broad asymmetric wings, no star or magic wand.
6. A birthday candle: one broad wax column with a distinct teardrop flame and no rays, the instant before making a wish.
7. A handwritten initial gesture: a distinctive free-standing single brushstroke double valley suggesting a W, not a typeset monogram, no tile, no serif w, no generic swoosh.
8. A personal dedication: two bold typographic opening quotation marks making a paired organic seed-like motif, no speech bubble.
9. A budding wish: one folded leaf opening around a single seed, wide simple negative space, not a generic two-leaf eco sprout.
Avoid every rejected direction: no rust square with w, no red rounded-square serif w, no tag with w, no speech bubble with heart, no ribbon bow or gift box, no star of any kind, no settings gear, no gradients, no glitter, no confetti, no existing-brand reference or imitation.
Composition: one professional flat exploration sheet, numbered 1-9, consistent visual size and distinct silhouettes. Each mark separated by plenty of white, no boxes, no shadows, no mockup effects.
